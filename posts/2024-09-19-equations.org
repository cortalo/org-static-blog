#+begin_hide
For a straight line $|z-a_1| = |z-a_2|$, we want to find the center of the circle $|\frac{1}{w}-a_1|=|\frac{1}{w}-a_2|$, we use
#+begin_latex
\begin{aligned}
w=u+jv, \quad a_1 = p+jq, \quad a_2 = r+js
\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}
\left(\dfrac{u}{u^2+v^2}-p\right)^2 + \left(\dfrac{v}{u^2+v^2}+q\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{u}{u^2+v^2}-r\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{v}{u^2+v^2}+s\right)^2
\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}
-\dfrac{2up}{u^2+v^2}+p^2+\dfrac{2vq}{u^2+v^2}+q^2=-\dfrac{2ur}{u^2+v^2} + r^2 + \dfrac{2vs}{u^2+v^2}+s^2
\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}
(u^2+v^2)(p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2)+2u(r-p)+2v(q-s)=0
\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}
u^2 + \dfrac{2(r-p)}{p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2}u + v^2 + \dfrac{2(q-s)}{p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2}v = 0
\end{aligned}
#+end_latex
The center of the circle locates
#+begin_latex
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{p-r}{p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2} -j \dfrac{q-s}{p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2}=\dfrac{(a_1-a_2)^*}{|a_1|^2-|a_2|^2}
\end{aligned}
#+end_latex
