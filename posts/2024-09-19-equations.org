#+begin_latex

\dfrac{-2\mu\alpha^* j}{|\beta + \alpha j|^2 - |\beta - \alpha j|^2} + \lambda

#+begin_hide
For a straight line $|z-a_1| = |z-a_2|$, we want to find the center of the circle $|\frac{1}{w}-a_1|=|\frac{1}{w}-a_2|$, we use
#+begin_latex
\begin{aligned}
w=u+jv, \quad a_1 = p+jq, \quad a_2 = r+js
\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}
\left(\dfrac{u}{u^2+v^2}-p\right)^2 + \left(\dfrac{v}{u^2+v^2}+q\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{u}{u^2+v^2}-r\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{v}{u^2+v^2}+s\right)^2
\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}
-\dfrac{2up}{u^2+v^2}+p^2+\dfrac{2vq}{u^2+v^2}+q^2=-\dfrac{2ur}{u^2+v^2} + r^2 + \dfrac{2vs}{u^2+v^2}+s^2
\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}
(u^2+v^2)(p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2)+2u(r-p)+2v(q-s)=0
\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}
u^2 + \dfrac{2(r-p)}{p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2}u + v^2 + \dfrac{2(q-s)}{p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2}v = 0
\end{aligned}
#+end_latex
The center of the circle locates
#+begin_latex
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{p-r}{p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2} -j \dfrac{q-s}{p^2+q^2-r^2-s^2}=\dfrac{(a_1-a_2)^*}{|a_1|^2-|a_2|^2}
\end{aligned}
#+end_latex
now for $\frac{1}{\alpha z + \beta}$, the line $\alpha z + \beta$ we can choose $a_1 = j\alpha + \beta, a_2 = -j\alpha + \beta$, the center for $\frac{1}{\alpha z + \beta}$ is
#+begin_latex
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{-2j\alpha^*}{|\beta+\alpha j|^2 - |\beta-\alpha j|^2}
\end{aligned}
#+end_latex
then the center for $w = \lambda + \dfrac{\mu}{\alpha z + \beta}$ is
#+begin_latex
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{-2\mu\alpha^*j}{|\beta+\alpha j|^2 - |\beta-\alpha j|^2} + \lambda
\end{aligned}

#+begin_latex
\begin{align}
Z_c = -\dfrac{2}{g_m} - \dfrac{n}{j\omega C_s}
\end{align}
\begin{align}
Y &= 1/Z_c = \dfrac{1}{-\dfrac{2}{g_m} - \dfrac{n}{j\omega C_s}}\\
&= -\dfrac{g_m j \omega C_s}{n g_m + 2j\omega C_s}\\
&= -\dfrac{2g_m\omega^2 C_s^2}{n^2g_m^2 + 4\omega^2 C_s^2} - j \dfrac{ng_m^2\omega C_s}{n^2 g_m^2 + 4\omega^2 C_s^2}
\end{align}

#+begin_latex

\end{align}
\begin{align}
\omega = \dfrac{ng_m}{2C_s}\sqrt{\dfrac{C_s}{nC_L}-1}
\end{align}
